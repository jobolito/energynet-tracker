An existing village-sized microgrid in California is being planned for an EnergyNet upgrade. Rather than replacing the installed generation and storage, EnergyNet is layered on top to add energy routing, sharing between properties and rule-based coordination that the current microgrid cannot do.

The project is in the planning stage. The parties have asked for it to be listed at state level only; the pin marks the state, not the site.
