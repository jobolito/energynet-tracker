A district-scale brownfield redevelopment in New York is being planned around an EnergyNet architecture. The aim is to use local production, storage and sharing between properties to increase resilience against outages and to keep energy costs down for residents.

The project is in the planning stage. The parties have asked for it to be listed by city only.
