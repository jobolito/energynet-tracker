A campus in Texas is planning an EnergyNet deployment with two goals: to accelerate expertise in local energy systems among the student body, with the installation serving as a living lab, and to improve campus resilience through local production, storage and sharing between buildings.

The project is in the planning stage. The parties have asked for it to be listed at state level only; the pin marks the state, not the campus.
